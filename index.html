How does the 100% insurance coverage work, and what does it cover?
Our 100% insurance coverage is designed to provide complete peace of mind when booking and hosting events through our platform.
It covers any damages to the venue during your event, as well as cancellation fees, should your event need to be canceled due to unforeseen circumstances.
To activate the insurance, simply opt-in during the booking process. There are no extra forms to fill out—our platform takes care of everything, ensuring you're automatically covered from the moment your booking is confirmed.
Note that terms and conditions apply, and we encourage all our users to review these details to understand the full scope of coverage.
